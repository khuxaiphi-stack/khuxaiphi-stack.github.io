# How to Host Your 1-Page Portfolio & Pilot Booking Site for Free (2 Minutes)

Your entire site is a single self-contained file: `index.html`. No build tools, databases, or servers are required.

---

## Option A: Netlify Drop (Fastest — 30 Seconds, No Coding)
1. Go to [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) and create a free account.
2. Drag and drop the **`portfolio-site`** folder onto the browser page.
3. Netlify immediately gives you a live HTTPS URL (e.g., `huzaifa-msp-runbooks.netlify.app`) that you can paste into your LinkedIn profile and email signature.

---

## Option B: Cloudflare Pages (Free, Fast Global CDN)
1. Log in to [dash.cloudflare.com](https://dash.cloudflare.com) > **Workers & Pages** > **Create** > **Pages** > **Upload assets**.
2. Name your project (e.g., `huzaifa-runbooks`), upload the `portfolio-site` folder, and deploy.

---

## Option C: GitHub Pages (Free)
1. Create a public GitHub repository (e.g., `msp-runbooks`).
2. Upload `index.html` to the root of the repository.
3. Go to **Settings** > **Pages** > **Deploy from a branch** (`main` / `/(root)`).

---

## Optional Upgrade Later
* If you create a free **Cal.com** or **Calendly** account later for 15-minute discovery calls, you can replace the `mailto:` action or add your booking link inside the `#book` section of `index.html`.
